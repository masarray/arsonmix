## Architecture
NoiseProfile owns candidate, trusted anchor and working PSD. Ignore two overlapping frames after Learn; reject gross energy deviation independently per channel, commit only a valid complete candidate. A rejected/canceled transaction preserves prior trusted/working profiles. Moderate drift tracking starts after existing 180 ms foreground guard plus an additional 500 ms idle hold, 2 s time constant, per-bin observation cap 8x anchor, working PSD bounded to 70–130% of anchor. ContextGuard always compares to immutable anchor to prevent self-reinforcing classification. Tracking stops on foreground, invalid profile, learning or transparent bypass. Large drift requires manual Learn. These are conservative heuristic policies; stationary music can still contaminate manual learning and quiet foreground may be mistaken for idle. Not a calibrated classifier. Fixed arrays, one DSP owner, no new FFT or latency. In exact-unity active Auto frames, retain analysis and context, skip inverse transform and gain computation using algebraic unity overlap-add.
