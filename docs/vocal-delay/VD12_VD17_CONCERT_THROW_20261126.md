## Architecture

Wet-only 640ms stereo history (preallocated) replays the selected 120–220ms segment with 8ms edge fades into existing stereo/tempo delay. Detector intentionally conservative; misses first uncalibrated climax and may be fooled by coherent noise/music. Input must be isolated singer channel. Manual is fallback.
