## Nonnegotiable product direction

**ArSonMiX is a native real-time LIVE digital mixer + user-chosen VST3 host**, for live stage/karaoke, podcasts, broadcast/streaming, hardware and user-selected virtual/physical destinations. Target Studio One/Fender Studio Pro **Console** feature parity, not timeline editing, arrangement/recording or copied proprietary code/GUI. No OBS/virtual-cable dependency, no fake output pins, no unbounded active-lane claims or measured-zero-millisecond round trip. Keep premium compact steel GUI, stable fader geometry, low-end iGPU interaction and ONE JUCE output mix callback; no second WebAudio DSP graph.
