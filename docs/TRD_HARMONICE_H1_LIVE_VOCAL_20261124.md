## H3 — musical ensemble voicing (owner listening feedback 24 Nov)

H1 45-case RMS gate did not prove individual voice F0 or chord compatibility. Audit: VoicePlanner independently scores each register, so scale membership alone admits adjacent seconds and non-triad stacks; slot MID sits on center lead; upper/lower support panning too close to outer anchors. Key/scale guide is not chord recognition. Industry references: [TC-Helicon VoiceLive 3 manual](https://manualzz.com/doc/8333295/tc-helicon-voicelive-3-reference-manual), [Antares Harmony Engine](https://www.antarestech.com/products/creative-vocal-effects/harmony-engine), [DAFX chapter 6 PSOLA](https://www.dafx.de/DAFX_Book_Page_2nd_edition/chapter6.html), [Open Music Theory voicings](https://pressbooks.nebraska.edu/openmusictheory/chapter/jazz-voicings/). Duo and Doubler unchanged. Upper support is octave of lead, not a new independent chord tone; intentional doubling distinct from crowding. Allocate normalized role power toward HIGH; stagger stage anchors (LOW -0.94, lower -0.49, MID -0.17, upper +0.43, HIGH +0.95) without new delay lines, preserve Space0 and existing PDC. Validate scale/triad/spacing across keys/history, isolated HIGH actual frequency and relative PCM energy at 110/220/440 Hz 48/96k, H1 absolute stem gate, prior Tune/dry/mono/block parity. No claims of human vocal naturalness until real singer A/B, and no full chord-context inference from key-only input. If pitch-synchronous HIGH remains unnatural in human test, isolate source/renderer with stems before changing synthesis or formants; never hide it with gain. No latency or GUI/state schema changes.
