# ArSonMix v0.5.0-alpha.24 — ImmerSIB + approved SonKu P9.9 (Windows public alpha)

**Scope:** Public, free-to-test **Windows 10/11 x64 alpha preview**, not a production/ASIO hardware certification. Published assets are created only if the exact-SHA publisher passes its real Windows build, package, installer/upgrade/uninstall, checksum and provenance gates. This release includes the approved `main` integration of PR #734 and preserves all previously published alpha.23 mixer and effect capabilities.

## What is new and reconciled

- **ImmerSIB spatial built-in FX:** user-facing name **ImmerSIB**, stable session/catalog identity `armix.builtin.imersip.v1`, seven named concert scenes: Live Concert, Royal Concert Hall, Arena Thunder, Front Row Power, Symphonic Palace, Velvet Cinema and Concert Front Row XL. The six 2200×922 concert venue PNG scenes are embedded into the real JUCE editor; Front Row XL intentionally reuses Live Concert's photograph. There is no alternate DSP renderer.
- **One native PCM engine:** ImmerSIB uses the existing SonKuSpatialProcessor/SonKuSpatialCore measured-HRTF foundation. Product-scoped warm/detail/impact and P9.9 vocal/room work are restricted to ImmerSIB; ordinary 8D-SonKu cannot accidentally load ImmerSIB-only voicing, nearfield or >100% Early Room via host JSON, PCM edit queue or SONK restore.
- **8D-SonKu P9.9:** preserve the user-approved immersive Orbit sound and protected Venue Mega Space reference, with no accidental transfer of ImmerSIB's private scene tuning into existing SonKu instances. Do not replace previously accepted SonKu settings on restored sessions.
- **Native control and session:** eight concert controls, exact unrounded factory presets through audio-applied ACK, Custom only after genuine edits, Source Distance 1.1–2.5 m and Early Room up to 180%. Native floating FX editor survives reopen; actual host slot/rack/global power controls determine ON/BYPASSED. A retired processor lease displays FX UNAVAILABLE, not a misleading active concert.
- **Session compatibility:** existing `.smix` Save/Open, IMRS v6/v7 and nested SONK v11/v12 identities remain. **Important:** older ImmerSIB distances outside the 1.1–2.5 m product range are clamped when restored. Back up valuable historical sessions before opening them with this alpha; no irreversible in-place session rewrite is required by the installer.
- **All earlier mixer and FX progress retained:** WIRE/BUS/MAIN routing, hardware channel/FX rack state, Harmonice, Guitar FX, Vocal Delay, EQ, Compressor, ArSon Player, native VST3 hosting and the previously merged approved console UI are not replaced by this ImmerSIB integration. Unmerged experimental Guitar FX redesign and unrelated research branches are not presented as shipped.

## Known alpha limitations and field checks

- Software/native PCM tests **do not prove** zero XRUNs, every ASIO/WASAPI driver, every audio interface, or preferred externalization on each listener's headphones. Hardware and matched-loudness listening remain operator field checks. No guarantee is made that an FX-on scene will never trigger its linked peak limiter on highly mastered material.
- Compare Live Concert, Concert Front Row XL and 8D-SonKu at matched perceived volume. Evaluate natural vocal body without piercing 2–4 kHz, kick/sub impact, instrument/treble width, audible room depth, phase stability, and absence of pumping. Test FX slot/rack/global bypass, editor reopen, device disconnect/reconnect and `.smix` reopen.
- ImmerSIB's current source-distance policy is **1.1–2.5 m**, not the earlier 1–4 m request; do not assume older settings are perceptually identical after migration.
- This Windows alpha does not claim full Linux/macOS release parity, certified hardware ASIO monitoring, or universal VST3 compatibility. Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues) for reproducible problems; provide device name, rate, buffer, scene and steps.
