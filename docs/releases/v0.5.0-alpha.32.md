# ArSonMix v0.5.0-alpha.32 — ImmerSIB Fixed PA / Refined Clarity

This **Windows x64 public alpha preview** promotes the owner-listened ImmerSIB P102.3 Refined Clarity sound to the official mixer. It includes the existing alpha.31 compact mixer/status-bar behavior and the single approved `/preview/` WebView2 GUI. This is **not** a physical multi-device-ASIO certification or a new I/O engine release.

## What you can hear

- **ImmerSIB P102.3**: the owner's preferred fixed two-speaker (Fixed PA) stage, frontal HRIR and concert room are preserved, with a conservative seven-filter **common L/R musical EQ**. The familiar spacious stage, warm vocal body, separation around 310 Hz, subtle presence near 5 kHz and air around 10.5 kHz remain the accepted checkpoint.
- **No moving mid/side spectral masking** from failed research profiles; no new room or distance algorithm after listening approval; no changes to general 8D-SonKu's separate product selection.
- **No double EQ needed:** if you saved an extra Parametric EQ for compensating older ImmerSIB versions, start with that external EQ bypassed and then apply only if you prefer its sound. The spatial profile is compiled into the ImmerSIB built-in effect.
- Existing approved mixer layout, FX rack, channel controls, scenes, native ACK/readback and `.smix` session format are retained. No second GUI or second realtime graph.

## Safe operation and known boundaries

- **Back up important `.smix` sessions first.** Test at low output volume and choose the desired primary ASIO or Windows Audio route intentionally. The sound calibration was performed at 44.1/48 kHz; headroom and listening response depend on programme material and device.
- Intermittent Windows Audio capture-input enumeration/recovery experienced during P100 testing is tracked separately in [PR #861](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/pull/861). Its diagnostic enhancements are **not** claimed as included in this release; do not present the I/O lifecycle bug as fixed. If it recurs, use **More → Copy Audio Report** *before restarting*.
- One primary ASIO device is the qualified architecture. Dual-vendor ASIO, hardware two-device synchronization, new G-MV/N-MOD/multi-I/O branches, unmerged graphic EQ and Guitar FX previews are **outside** this release scope.
- No claim of field-tested acoustic quality on all headphones, physical analogue latency or two-vendor ASIO hardware FAT. Windows x64 binary/installer qualification is separate from owner acoustic approval. Linux and macOS require their own genuine native build and platform qualification.
- Previous alpha.31 installation remains available as a rollback. Preserve prior settings and listen at matched perceived loudness when comparing revisions.
