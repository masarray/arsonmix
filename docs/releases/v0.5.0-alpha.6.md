# ArSonMix v0.5.0-alpha.6 — Native Compressor, Studio LCD meters & mixer polish

> **PUBLIC ALPHA RELEASE CANDIDATE — NOT YET PUBLISHED.** Community testing only; not certified for live-stage use. Existing [alpha.5](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/releases/tag/v0.5.0-alpha.5) must remain intact until the immutable alpha.6 candidate is qualified and explicitly promoted.

## Release scope — reconciled with main at `6f2daf978ae2c3a409ad38a5eab148f6260154b8`

- **Built-in native Compressor** (`armix.builtin.channel-compressor.v1`) as an ordered insert on the authoritative JUCE / ChannelId PCM graph. Stereo-linked feed-forward dynamics with Threshold, Ratio, Knee, Attack, Release, Makeup and Mix; session restore and instance/graph revision-gated control ACK/readback. No lookahead and **zero additional compressor sample latency**; this is *not* zero audio device/driver latency.
- **Professional 795px Compressor GUI matching built-in Parametric EQ scale**, with a header preset dropdown, clean large black compression LCD, drag-to-edit threshold, and three *real* PCM meters on the right edge of that LCD: Input and Output in dBFS rise from the bottom; Gain Reduction starts at 0 dB at the top and grows downward. Meters expire after a stopped/changed PCM block serial rather than suggesting frozen audio is live.
- **Global close/power UX.** Standard Close chrome, red on press, and native-state-authoritative Power colors: active, insert bypass, rack bypass, and unavailable. Don't confuse a button click with a confirmed PCM graph state.
- **Original metal fader thumbs retained.** The approved channel/master PNG geometry remains unchanged. The owner-approved **static** shadow is `0 20px 10px -1px #000c, 0 4px 3px -4px #000a`; there is no CSS filter, shadow animation, or new per-frame JavaScript work.
- **Single GUI/source authority.** The installed JUCE WebView and public `/preview/` use the approved assembled GUI. No competing audio-render authority is introduced.

## Explicit exclusions and known risks

- **ArSon Harmony** PR [#467](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/pull/467), **Vocal Throw** PR [#469](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/pull/469) and stacked music-tempo PR [#474](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/pull/474) are **NOT in this candidate**: still-draft research/engineering qualification and unverified on the owner’s physical ASIO installation. Harmony’s stated 32ms additional latency at 48k is a release risk for live-ear singing.
- Input Gain, lookahead, Auto Gain/Release and external Sidechain are not active Compressor controls. Internal stereo-linked detection only.
- Bonus FactoryFX VST3 packages are optional and included **only when each exact approved external bundle/identity and integrity are independently proven**; the earlier verified package reported no extra FactoryFX. Native EQ and Compressor do not depend on them.
- Outstanding field blockers: [hardware/DAW flow #412](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/412), [audio crackle #15](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/15), [dynamic channels #192](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/192), [Show restore #180](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/180), [native GUI parity #389](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/389), [FactoryFX bundles #185](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/185), and [OS distribution #453](https://github.com/masarray/arsonmix/issues/453).
- Green Chromium, native CTest, Inno Setup packaging and provenance checks are **software verification only**. They do not establish ASIO crackle-free performance, safe monitor latency, real-singer musical quality, or hardware qualification.
