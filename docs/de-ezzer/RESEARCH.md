# Research and provenance — 2026-11-25
- FabFilter Pro-DS official manual https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-ds/intro/about and https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-ds/using/advancedcontrols : source-oriented detection, wide versus split processing, stereo link, optional lookahead. Wideband can preserve timbre while modulating whole signal; linear-phase split trades delay for selectivity. Product reference only, proprietary internals unknown.
- Jongman, Wayland & Wong (2000) https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11008825/ : fricative spectral peak, spectral moments, relative/normalized amplitude help distinguish classes. Motivates multi-band relative detector rather than fixed HF threshold; not a calibrated classifier or proof of singing detection.
- Giannoulis, Massberg & Reiss (2012) https://aes.org/publications/elibrary/elibrary-page/?id=16354 : compressor detector, gain curve and timing design. Motivates asymmetric smoothing and bounded gain. Original C++ implementation, no source copied.
- Universal Audio Precision De-Esser https://help.uaudio.com/hc/en-us/articles/33536699791892-Precision-De-Esser-Manual : split processing and high-end control on individual tracks/mix. 
Further research: adaptive reference, robust spectral distribution, voiced fricatives, matched-benefit listening, low-latency filter alternatives. Need licensed/owner-authorized speech/singing corpus, Indonesian/English, annotated sibilants, protected instruments and tails; separate development and held-out sets. Controlled excess-HF augmentation can supply paired reference; natural recordings do not have a unique clean target. No copyrighted corpus committed.
