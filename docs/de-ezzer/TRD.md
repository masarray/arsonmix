# de-Ezzer — technical contract DS-01
Core::prepare(rate,channels): finite 44100..96000 Hz, mono/stereo only, rejects invalid without replacing previous state; reset clears histories. Settings finite normalized amount/sensitivity [0,1], range [0,18] dB, enabled bool. Invalid packet rejected atomically by audio owner. No shared-thread access. In-place valid nonoverlapping channel buffers. Nonfinite input sanitized to zero. Double internal states, float PCM, fixed memory, no buffer/FFT/lookahead.
 
Detector: constant-peak-gain bandpass biquads at 950 Hz Q=.55, 4600 and 8000 Hz Q=1.1. Per-channel squared envelope coefficient 1-exp(-1/(rate*1.2ms)). Every eight samples, max HF band / body energy, floor 1e-10 power, threshold 2-12*sensitivity dB. Max nonnegative excess across channels; no summed-mono cancellation. Reduction target min(range,1.35*amount*excess), linear amplitude 10^(-dB/20). Attack .65 ms, release 45 ms in amplitude domain. Initial 1 ms detector warmup avoids startup filter transient; early sibilance can be missed. Control tick phase persisted across blocks. Lowpass state L one-pole matched exponential at 3200 Hz; output = g*x+(1-g)*L. Gain applies to complementary residual, not exact measured full-spectrum reduction. No-trigger/zero/disabled converges to exact finite dry (snap 1e-7); transitions intentionally smoothed, not instant hard bypass. No peak growth beyond finite input history by convex blend. Flush tiny states; no fast-math. 
Limits: no adaptive reference, harmonic/phoneme classifier, source-role modes, targeted EQ, independent link, delta renderer or native command/GUI. Bright instruments, HF hiss and low-body sibilance may trigger; DS-02 must measure and improve. Current frequency response changes somewhat with sample rate; measure true complex gain, not just in-phase projection. DS-01 synthetic fixture uses in-phase projection, not full energy, phase-independent attenuation or listening evidence. Future quality gates: real held-out labeled events, precision/recall and false-trigger duration, level invariance, weak consonants, singing, cymbals, stereo, phase and transient tests. Physical ASIO and integrated graph qualification separate.

## DS-02 amendment (supersedes DS-01 clock/threshold)
Independent narrow 600Hz Q1.5 witness, 85ms decaying peak memory, 120ms bounded relative reference. Control period round(rate/6000), persisted; sensitivity threshold cached linear; adaptive threshold max(user threshold,min(user threshold+5dB, reference+2dB in dB domain)). Authority ramps with bodyMemory/HF .012..04. Adapt only HF/body<=1.3 and current witness>=.08 HF. See DS-02-EVIDENCE.md. No trained classifier, adaptive frequency or source modes yet.

## DS-03A amendment
Settings adds validated Mode::Auto (default) and Mode::Voice (explicit vocal intent, not classification). Voice adds a bounded two-lag prediction-error cue to allow HF-dominant noise-like bursts without prior body. Baseline Auto unchanged. Predictor states reset on mode change/reset, gain remains smoothed. See DS-03A-EVIDENCE.md for equations, measurements and limitations. No additional sample delay or mandatory FFT. Offline float32 wet/Delta export implemented; native live audition and source-role selection pending.

## DS-03B amendment — optional Focused shape
Shape::Shelf is default; Shape::Focused is opt-in, separate from Mode Auto/Voice. Reuse the actual lower/upper detector bandpass samples, no extra filter or FFT. At control tick the strongest positive channel excess owns target weight (first channel wins exact tie); w=E_lower/(E_lower+E_upper+1e-25), p=w²/(w²+(1-w)²). Hold weight without positive evidence. Smooth weight and shape blend at 8ms, retain existing gain smoothing and range cap. Focused output x-(1-g)*(p*lower+(1-p)*upper), continuously crossfade to/from shelf. Fixed coefficients, same sample timing; zero added buffering, exact unity at settled gain=1. Frequency-dependent phase and time-varying transient behavior remain; GR is scalar attenuation authority, not actual dB at every frequency. Core snapshot focusLowerWeight is audio-owner only. Not a continuous frequency estimator or a calibrated sibilance classifier. See evidence for matched-benefit gates and known limitations.

## DS-03C amendment — stereo evidence fusion and faster targeting
Supersedes the DS-03B strongest-channel target selection and 8ms frequency smoothing. Keep maximum per-channel excess for linked scalar gain. For each positive-excess channel, use its existing squared-normalized lower-band weight; pool these weights proportionally to channel excess, clamp to [0,1], hold if no positive evidence. The same weight and gain drive both channels; no crossfeed and channel permutation invariance tested. Separate 2ms target-weight time constant from 8ms shape transition. No additional filter, FFT, delay or allocation introduced. Target weight settling is not a claim of actual fricative onset attenuation; two simultaneous bands share a bounded scalar attenuation budget. See DS-03C-EVIDENCE.md.
